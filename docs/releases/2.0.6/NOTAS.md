# Nuvio Legacy 2.0.6

Fixes and polish after 2.0.5. Community server: **https://discord.gg/9NWr6SHyzJ**

## Fixed

- **Subtitle delay sign.** The saved delay flips sign once on Android, webOS and .tpk, so a positive delay means later again.
- **Android TV (TCL).** Playback no longer closes the app from low memory.
- **LG: smoother screens.** Lighter glass and less overdraw on the main screens, including the profile screen.
- **Source audio language** (#529/#532) and **manual source choice on episodes** (#513/#519).
- **Seek progress** is persisted (#522); **regex** follow-ups (#489/#525).

## Added

- **Friends row.** Landscape posters and people cards.
- **Hero trailer.** Settings explain why the trailer toggle does not apply.
- **Remote tips.** Shown once per version.
- **Hide watched in Discover** (#366/#490), **profile session isolation** (#493), **keyboard search** (#498), **episode controls in the player** (#496/#534), **subtitle track names** (#511/#518), **synopsis text size** (#477/#526), **Android log download** (#520).
